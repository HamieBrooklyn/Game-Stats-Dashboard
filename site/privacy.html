Privacy policy

Game Stats · stats.pokepon.org · Updated 8 November 2026

Game Stats is a private, invite-only dashboard that shows the owner and invited team members the statistics of their own Roblox experiences. This page explains what it stores when you sign in with Roblox.

What we store

  • Your Roblox user ID, username and display name, from Roblox when you sign in.
  • Which of the dashboard’s experiences you chose to share on Roblox’s consent screen, so you only see those.
  • When you joined and when you last signed in.
  • A sign-in cookie that keeps you signed in for up to 7 days. It holds no personal information.

What we don’t store

Roblox’s access and refresh tokens are used once at sign-in, to read who you are and which experiences you shared, and are then revoked. The dashboard never uses them to read or change anything else, and never stores them. We don’t use analytics or advertising trackers, and we don’t sell or share your information.

Where it’s kept

On the owner’s own computer, which runs the dashboard. It’s reached through Cloudflare, which carries the traffic.

How long

Until the owner removes you, or you ask to be removed. You can also revoke the app’s access at any time in your Roblox account settings, under Security → Third-party app permissions.

Contact

Ask the person who invited you, or open an issue at github.com/HamieBrooklyn/Game-Stats-Dashboard.
